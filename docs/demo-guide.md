# Shortlyst — Demo Video & GIF Recording Guide

This guide details the ideal 15–20 second recording sequence for generating `docs/demo.gif` for your hackathon submission.

---

## 📹 Recording Specifications

- **Target Output File**: `docs/demo.gif`
- **Ideal Duration**: 15 to 20 seconds
- **Recommended Resolution**: 1280×720 or 1920×1080 (downscaled to ~1000px width GIF)
- **Recommended Tool**: [Loom](https://loom.com), [ScreenToGif](https://www.screentogif.com), [CleanShot X](https://cleanshot.com), or OBS Studio.

---

## 🎬 18-Second Recording Storyboard

| Timestamp | Screen | Action to Record |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00 – 0:04** | **Landing Page (`/`)** | Hover over hero text & background video, click **Start Screening Candidates** button. |
| **0:04 – 0:07** | **Login Page (`/login`)** | Enter email/password (or toggle to **Sign Up**), click **Sign In to Screener**. |
| **0:07 – 0:12** | **Screener Page (`/app`)** | Paste sample Job Description into textarea, drag 2 sample PDF resumes into the Upload Zone, click **Screen Candidates**. |
| **0:12 – 0:15** | **Loading Shimmer** | Show rotating status banner and shimmering card skeletons while Gemini evaluates. |
| **0:15 – 0:20** | **Results List** | Scroll through ranked candidate cards, hover over score circle, and click **Export as CSV**. |

---

## 💾 Saving & Compression Steps

1. Save or convert your recorded video to GIF format at `docs/demo.gif`.
2. Keep file size under **8 MB** for fast rendering on GitHub README.
3. Recommended free GIF optimizer: [ezgif.com/optimize](https://ezgif.com/optimize).
